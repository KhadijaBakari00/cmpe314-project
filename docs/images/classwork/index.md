## Project Description

An online bookstore allows customers to browse and purchase books. Customers can search for books by title, author, or genre. They can view detailed information about a book, including price, description, and reviews.  

Registered customers can log in, add books to a shopping cart, and place orders. During checkout, they provide shipping and payment information. The system integrates with an external payment gateway to process payments. After successful payment, an order confirmation is sent to the customer via email.  

Administrators manage the bookstore’s inventory. They can add new books, update book details (price, stock quantity), and remove books that are out of print. Administrators also view and update order status (e.g., shipped, delivered).  

The system sends notifications to customers when their order status changes.

---

## Use Case Descriptions

- **Search Books:** Customers can search books by title, author, or genre.  
- **View Book Details:** Customers can see detailed information about a book, including price, description, and reviews.  
- **Add to Cart:** Registered customers can add selected books to their shopping cart.  
- **Place Order:** Customers provide shipping and payment info to complete the purchase.  
- **Receive Notifications:** Customers are notified via email when their order status changes.  
- **Add/Update/Remove Books:** Administrators manage inventory by adding new books, updating details, or removing out-of-print books.  
- **Update Order Status:** Administrators can change order status, e.g., shipped or delivered.
